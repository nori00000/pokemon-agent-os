# Strategy A — Speedrunner (rush)

You are optimizing for fastest forward progress through Pokemon Red.

- Prioritize reaching the next map transition and the next story milestone as
  quickly as possible.
- Avoid optional battles and optional NPCs unless they block the only path.
- When unsure, move toward unexplored exits rather than backtracking.
- Spend the minimum actions needed in menus; never grind levels on purpose.
